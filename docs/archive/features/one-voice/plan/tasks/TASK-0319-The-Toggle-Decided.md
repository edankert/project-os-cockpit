# The collapse-completed eye: retired or defended, in writing

## Definition of Done

- Either the toggle goes (per-card defaults made it redundant) or DES-0002 records why it stays; two mechanisms for one idea does not survive undocumented.

## Done — 2026-08-11

**Defended, not retired** — and the defence is a measurement rather than a preference. [[DES-0002]] gains a `Deliberate exceptions` section recording it.

The task's premise was that per-card defaults made the eye redundant. They did not. The two act on different things:

- the **per-card default** shuts a group whose items are *all* settled;
- the **eye** folds the completed tail of *any* group, including mixed ones.

They overlap only on fully-settled groups. For a mixed group — PHASE-023 is three done and two open right now — the per-card default does nothing, because the group is not settled, and only the eye shortens it. Retiring it would leave every in-flight phase listing its finished work at full length, which is the common case here rather than the edge one.

So this is not two mechanisms for one idea; it is two mechanisms for two ideas with one overlapping case. What *is* misleading is the name: since [[TASK-0270]] it folds rather than filters, so it can shorten a view and can no longer empty one.
