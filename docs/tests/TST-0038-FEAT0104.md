# Every acceptance row carries its mark and its address

Automated, in `tests/test_acceptance_marks.py`.

## What it pins

**That all four marks are addressed, not just the two `pymdownx.tasklist` understands.** That extension renders `[~]` and `[F]` with no input element at all, so an HTML checkbox can never carry the record's vocabulary — which is the real reason [[FEAT-0104]] stalled, and not the [[ISS-0175]] lazy continuation its own note recorded.

**That the priority ordering holds.** The treeprocessor is registered at 26, above `task-list` at 25. Below it, only `~` and `F` get addressed — the exact inverse of what is wanted, which a first pass produced.

**That one regex reads one convention.** The matcher uses `acceptance._NAME_RE`; a hand-rolled split on the first colon left 70 of 579 rows unaddressed because a name may contain one.

**That the shortfall is stated at its measured number.** 6 of 579 on the living suite, 36 of 300 on the v2.1.0 delta, nothing at all on this repo's. Asserted exactly, so it cannot grow unnoticed.
