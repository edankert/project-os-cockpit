The standing worker

The loop, the lease, the policy it consults, and every way it stops

One turn
can start?→ claim the lease→ select, with reasons→ dispatch→ record the outcome→ release

The lease is released on every path, including a raising dispatcher — a worker that died holding it would block the next one until the heartbeat aged out.

States
idle
no workable item — a stop condition, not a busy-wait
working
lease held · budget counting · one item
escalated
waiting on a person — lapses to a recorded assumption, or alarms
halted
files what and why — an obligation on the desk, not an absence

Every halt carries a reason. A halt with no reason is indistinguishable from a worker that merely went quiet — and only one of those is fine.

What a selection records

passed over: TASK-3: status done is not workable; TASK-4: blocked on TASK-9; TASK-2: ranked below the choice

“Why not that one?” is the question a person actually asks when a choice looks wrong, and the choice alone cannot answer it.
