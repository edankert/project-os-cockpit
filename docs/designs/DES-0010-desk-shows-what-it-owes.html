E The card, and why it needs the width

The gain over the list is not the columns. It is that a card can carry what a 240 px row truncates at nineteen characters.

Today · the 240 px row
0029A delegate is always dist…

Id and nineteen characters. Type, state, phase and age are in the title tooltip — which a tablet cannot reach, on a server that binds 0.0.0.0 precisely so a tablet can read it.
